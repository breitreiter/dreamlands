# Color bible — The Villa

This file holds **only recurring motifs**: the few textures the arc returns
to across scenes. They are available to any scene's grab bag where they fit,
rendered fresh and never forced; they are the threads that tie the five
scenes into one arc.

**What does NOT belong here:** per-scene and per-paragraph color, i.e. any
detail that appears in only one place (the dried herbs, the key on the cord,
the seated corpse, the coded shorthand, the formation-goods column, the cold
brazier, the body in the cabinet, the smell of burnt paper, the cave's
after-image). colorize generates that per scene from the lens and the beats.
The test for inclusion: *do we come back to it?* If not, it is not a motif.

The per-scene lens sets each scene's stance; these motifs are the shared
substance underneath it.

Format: each `- ` entry is a motif. The bold phrase + sentence is the
canonical invariant (keep it fixed); the `render:` line is how to instance
it fresh. A `only-in:` line scopes a callback to named scenes; absent means
available everywhere.

Register note: Tier 1 scrub, first contact. The one supernatural motif (the
crystals) is faint and isolated, never saturated. Render the glow small.

## Recurring motifs

- **the faded splendor** — the villa was a fine, worldly house once, elegant
  in its bones, and was already run-down when Solan bought it cheap from an
  owner who needed cash more than the house. Good materials and fine
  proportions gone to ruin; beauty visible underneath the wreck. We return
  to it room by room as the PC moves through the house.
  render: an elegant feature now ruined in this exact spot (a patterned tile
  floor dulled under grime, a carved lintel or doorframe, fine plaster
  water-stained over good work, a once-good room let go). The grandeur and
  the decay in the same detail.

- **the narrowed house** — on top of the long ruin, Solan's own recent
  squalor: a man who boarded himself in for months and reduced his footprint
  room by room until he occupied only the study and the cave below it. The
  third age stacked on the first two.
  render: a sign of the contraction in this exact room (windows shuttered
  from inside, weeks of dust, a bed slept on one side, the mess of one man
  living alone in a corner of a house too big for him).

- **the journal as an object** — the physical artifact the PC carries
  through the whole middle of the arc: a journal bound inside a ledger's
  cover, written in a different hand than the records, its pages loose and
  some stuck together and some water-damaged at the edges, read out of
  order. A thing handled, not just read.
  render: a tactile detail of the book in *this* moment (a page that will
  not come free of the next, a damaged edge, the weight of it set down or
  carried, the wrong-feeling fact of a journal hidden inside a ledger).

- **the changing hand** — Solan's writing degrades across the journal's
  life, and we track it scene to scene: dry and even at first, looser with
  the good days, tightening with the obsession, shrunk in places too fine to
  read, then not writing at all but geometric diagrams. The handwriting is
  the man.
  render: the state of the hand on *these* pages, matched to where in the
  descent the beat sits. The single most load-bearing journal motif.

- **the crystals** — geometrically perfect, faceted in patterns that do not
  occur in undisturbed rock, with a faint glow that is not a color the PC
  has a word for: a hue pushed past the end of what the eye knows, a
  more-than-purple. Looked at closely they seem to hold structure within
  structure, the same shape nested and approached from several sides at
  once. They recur as object, as the thing Solan draws, and as the cave
  itself. (Canon: `text/lore/scrub.md`, "the Colors" / the Lattice nodes.)
  render: one facet of them in this moment (the unnatural regularity in raw
  stone, the faint wrong glow, the nested structure, the failure to name the
  color). Keep the glow faint; never hand the PC the plain word "purple."

## Callbacks

- **the ordered pack** — in the keep ending, weeks later and east of the
  mesa, the PC wakes to a pack gone through in the night: not robbed but
  *ordered*, every item set where they did not put it and yet exactly where
  it belongs, the geometry of the arrangement correct in a way that is its
  own answer (the crystals' structure-within-structure, surfacing in the
  PC's own kit). The journal is not among the items. The arc's only
  supernatural payoff and the one place the crystal motif comes back as
  something that has touched the PC.
  only-in: The Decision
