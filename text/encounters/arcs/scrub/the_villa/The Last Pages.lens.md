# Lens — The Last Pages

How the PC sees this scene. Steering for color: whose eye, what to notice,
in what key. Stance, not facts.

## Perceptual
A reader at the back of the book, where the writing fails. First the eye is
still on the page: the hand shrunk in places too fine to read, the few flat
lines about staying below and not being hungry anymore, and then the final
page that is not text at all but geometric diagrams worked over many
sittings, solids drawn from impossible angles, "Not finished. Not close."
in the corner. Then, on the optional spoke, the eye leaves the book and
goes down the low door into the cave: the structured crystals, the very
faint light that is not a color you can name, what it does to the rock, what
it does at the corner of the eye. This is the only scene where the PC has
direct contact with the wrong thing. Render the supernatural faint and
exact, never lush.

## Social / tonal
Wonder shading immediately into wrongness, and this is the arc's one
genuinely supernatural register, kept small on purpose (Tier 1, first
contact). The failing journal pages are quiet horror: a man dissolving into
geometry, recorded in his own shrinking hand. The cave is the single beat
where dread becomes physical, the after-image that will not fade on
schedule, the `+damage_spirits` made felt. Hold the restraint the rest of
the arc has earned: the light is barely there, the crystals do not move or
sing, nothing reaches out. The horror is that an almost-ordinary cave is
not ordinary, and that the PC chose to go down and look.
