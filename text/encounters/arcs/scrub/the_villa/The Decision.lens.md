# Lens — The Decision

How the PC sees this scene. Steering for color: whose eye, what to notice,
in what key. Stance, not facts.

## Perceptual
The PC sets the journal down in the study and weighs what to do before
walking back out to the gate. The eye is inward and practical first: the
cold brazier in the corner, the five ledgers where they were found, the
loose pages that will not help tonight, the hour Vastand has left. Then, on
each branch, the eye goes to Vastand at the gate, read as a merchant reads a
counterparty: the careful way he turns pages and stops on the last one, the
arithmetic behind his face when he is told the cost, the smell of burnt
paper if it comes to that. In the keep branch the eye leaps forward weeks to
a roadside dawn and a pack that has been put in order by no hand the PC will
ever name. Notice the human and the transactional; the supernatural appears
only in the keep flash-forward, and even there it is restraint, an
arrangement too correct, not a haunting.

## Social / tonal
Social tension and quiet moral weight, the horror now living in the choice
rather than the dark. The register is a careful conversation with a careful
man under a clock: not hostile, genuinely dangerous in the giving-the-
journal branch because Vastand is intelligent and not immune. Each branch
has its own key, the clean scheme and its smell of smoke, the practical
arithmetic of telling him what it cost, the flat dread of the ordered pack.
Vastand is practical before he is moral and should read that way, neither
villain nor ally. Keep the cosmic note out of every branch but the keep
ending, and keep it faint even there: the cost of this arc is mostly what
the PC decides to do with what they know.
