# Lens — The Relay Post (Evening)

How the PC sees this scene. Steering for color: whose eye, what to notice,
in what key. Stance, not facts.

## Perceptual
The same stone room, now holding three people and the argument the PC walked
into, resuming worn smooth by long repetition. The eye reads the two of them
as a merchant reads two counterparties who have had this exact disagreement
many times: Devra with evidence and no framework, Ossal with a framework that
meets the evidence only where it chooses to. Read Ossal closely — old,
seventies at least, moving slowly to conserve energy, fifty years in Kesharat
service, a true believer who states cosmic claims (the Lattice, a topology
beyond three dimensions) as flatly and reasonably as a supply schedule. Notice
the small cross-room reactions: Devra exhaling at her desk when he reaches a
familiar phrase, having heard the speech before. On the re-read of the file,
the eye goes cold and careful: a routine maintenance notice carrying this
building's own coordinates, timestamped before the PC arrived, with no origin
header. The brass receiver ticks once in its cradle and goes quiet.

## Social / tonal
Social and conversational, a long evening boxed in by the storm. The register
is two intelligent, honest people talking past each other, the warmth between
them worn thin but not gone. This is the scene where the off-key note gets
*substantiated*: the predicted-arrival message is the seam that will not
close, and it lands in a room that is otherwise only tired and talkative. Not
horror yet — unease, the held breath of a wrong fact in ordinary paperwork.
Keep Ossal's cosmic claims something he believes and states calmly, never
narrated as true; the narrator does not name the strangeness, it lets the
plain maintenance notice do the work. Keep the supernatural faint; the dread
is administrative.
