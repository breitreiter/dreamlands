# Lens — Aftermath (building the story)

How the PC sees this scene. Steering for color — whose eye, what to notice, in
what key. Stance, not facts.

## Perceptual
A traveller standing at the circle's edge, watching four men who are themselves
again around a broken tower. The eye is on the small physical facts: the foreman
turning a piece of the broken unit in his hand, the young one finding a bruise on
his ribs he cannot account for, the wrecked housing, the cups still out. And on
the fourth man, quiet, watching his own hands.

## Social / tonal
Dread, and on the right thread, revelation: the crew assembles a false account in
real time — heat sickness, bad well water, the merchant came up from the south —
each contribution offered plainly and accepted, none of them lying, the story
feeling to them like remembering. Render the calm collaborative assembly as
observed speech and gesture. The one seam is the fourth man: if the thread gives,
he stops, looks at his hands, sets his cup down. Keep it to what shows.
