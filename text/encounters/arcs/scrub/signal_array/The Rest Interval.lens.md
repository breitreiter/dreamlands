# Lens — The Rest Interval (the circle)

How the PC sees this scene. Steering for color — whose eye, what to notice, in
what key. Stance, not facts.

## Perceptual
The same traveller, now seated. The eye is on the rest circle and its materials:
the kettle and small pot, four tin cups, the bedroll mat laid out on bare rock,
the kit produced from a pack. Plain issue — canvas, leather, brass, tin. The four
men in their fixed places: the foreman a little upslope, the young one beside
him, the heavy one across, the fourth off-axis. Look at hands, postures, how men
sit who have sat together a long time.

## Social / tonal
Warmth is the baseline here, aliveness under it: tea, the worst hour passing, the
crew at ease, the road quiet. You are a guest now. The one seam is the fourth
man, not quite settled into the circle the others close around him. Keep dread a
faint minority; this beat establishes the company before any spoke turns it.
Render the off-note as posture, never as a stated unease.
