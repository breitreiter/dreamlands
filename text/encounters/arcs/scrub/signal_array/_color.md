# Color bible — The Signal Array

Arc-wide recurring color: motifs always available to any scene's grab bag where
relevant (rendered fresh, never forced), plus a few hand-authored callbacks
scoped to the scenes where they land. Canonical by authorship. The per-scene
lens sets each scene's stance; this is the shared substance underneath it.

Format: each `- ` entry is a motif. The bold phrase + sentence is the canonical
invariant (keep it fixed); the `render:` line is how to instance it fresh.
A `only-in:` line scopes an entry to named scenes (used for callbacks so they
cannot land in an earlier scene); absent means available everywhere.

## Recurring motifs

- **the heat off the rock** — late afternoon, the sun lowering west; heat still
  radiating up out of the bedrock, the air only starting to slacken.
  render: how the heat sits in this exact spot and moment.

- **the smell of hot metal** — carried off the pylon's struts on a wind gust,
  gone again when the wind drops.
  render: catch it on a specific gust, or its absence in the still air.

- **the pylon** — a lattice of metal struts taller than a barn, guy-cables drawn
  taut into a cut in the rock; more worked iron standing open on the road than a
  village smith sees in a year. The PC has no word for what it is.
  render: in its current state (half-built and silent at arrival, complete and
  live later), through the renaissance eye.

- **the interval bell** — a brass case the size of a fist on Veran's belt,
  spring-wound; its ring a single hard clap and a sustained buzz. He never looks
  at it, before it rings or after.
  render: where it rides on the belt, the sound of it, or the weight of its silence.

- **the issued uniform** — dun canvas, a broad oiled-leather belt, a white neck
  cloth, and one small brass button at every throat stamped with the Kesharat
  mark, the only ornament. It reads unmistakably as issued, and it fits all four
  men built nothing alike.
  render: a detail of the kit on one man, or the brass button at a collar.

- **the tea kit** — a kettle, a small pot, four tin cups, a bedroll mat laid out
  on the bare rock; Baret has it out before Veran sits.
  render: a specific piece of it, in use or set down.

## Callbacks

- **the cups still out** — at the rest interval the four tin cups are set out on
  the rock. Re-notice them: the rest circle never struck, the cups still where
  they were poured.
  only-in: Aftermath.enc

- **the bell gone silent** — the interval bell structured the whole afternoon.
  Re-notice it by its silence here: no clap, no buzz, no next interval coming.
  only-in: Aftermath.enc

- **Baret's clan band** — established at arrival, the Reshîd band at his rolled
  cuff. Re-notice it as he checks his own ribs for the bruise he cannot account
  for, the band at his wrist.
  only-in: Aftermath.enc

- **the hum where there was none** — the half-built tower stood silent at arrival.
  Re-notice the change: the completed pylon humming on the rock, the sound it did
  not have before.
  only-in: Completion.enc
