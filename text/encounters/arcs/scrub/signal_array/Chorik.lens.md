# Lens — Chorik (the one who didn't flip)

How the PC sees this scene. Steering for color — whose eye, what to notice, in
what key. Stance, not facts.

## Perceptual
A traveller watching the fourth man up close — the most correctly uniformed of
the four, buttoned to the throat, neck cloth crisp, holding all of it too still
in the heat. The eye is on what the body does and does not do: the cup held in
both hands, the gaze gone to his own hands, the small distance he keeps inside a
small circle. He does not sweat through the uniform the way the others do.

## Social / tonal
The heaviest, quietest beat: dread, and on the unguarded turn, revelation. He
deflects, declines, sits inside the others' on-his-behalf lies without confirming
or denying. If he opens, it is fear named plainly and low — gaps, leaving and
never quite managing it, the mesa driving him mad — shut the instant the
foreman's voice crosses the rock. Keep color spare and close; substantiate the
stillness and the fear as observable, and let the others' patching be heard, not
explained.
