# Lens — Observe (the right shape)

How the PC sees this scene. Steering for color — whose eye, what to notice, in
what key. Stance, not facts.

## Perceptual
A traveller sitting at the circle's edge, not talking, just watching the crew be
a crew: the ribbing about the morning's work, the second round of tea poured, a
story that does not quite track, a laugh a beat behind from the fourth. The eye
is on the fit between them — the shorthand, the timing, the easy familiarity of
men weeks on the road together.

## Social / tonal
Aliveness tipping into dread: the familiarity is total, and that is the wrong
thing — they are the right shape and nothing else about them is. Where the
traveller carries the relay-post context, the watching turns to cold recognition
that the wire traffic was scheduling this. Render the company's easy fit as warm
observed detail; let the wrongness sit in how complete the fit is, not in a
clause about it.
