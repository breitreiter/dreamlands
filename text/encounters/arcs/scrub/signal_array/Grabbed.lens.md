# Lens — Grabbed (the patient hand)

How the PC sees this scene. Steering for color — whose eye, what to notice, in
what key. Stance, not facts.

## Perceptual
A traveller with a foreman's hand on the shoulder — not gripping hard, patient,
the way you steady someone who is unwell. The eye is on the physical moment: the
housing still right there, the latch plate bolted to the bedrock, the crew on the
work side not yet coordinated, the fourth man just starting to turn. Bodies,
weight, the distance to the housing.

## Social / tonal
Action under dread. The crew is not hostile and not yet a unit; the menace is the
unhurried firmness, men about to firm up who have not quite yet. Render the
struggle and the half-formed crew as observable physical detail — the hand, the
balance, the plate, the turn — and let the calm be the wrong thing, unargued.
