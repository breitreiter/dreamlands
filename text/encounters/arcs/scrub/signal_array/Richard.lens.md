# Lens — Richard (the farmer and the technician)

How the PC sees this scene. Steering for color — whose eye, what to notice, in
what key. Stance, not facts.

## Perceptual
A traveller who knows grasslands, farms, and pigs, listening to a heavy-built man
from the rich country near Aldgate worry about his prize sow and his brother's
husbandry. The eye is on the farmer: the grassland sunburn at the back of the
neck (open-pasture sun, not the dry-mesa burn the others wear), the hog-lifter's
build, the farmer's hands. Then his voice goes precise and clinical on the tower,
and the eye catches the seam between the two men in one body.

## Social / tonal
Warm pastoral worry, then a cold technical fluency that is correct-shaped and
wrong in small confident details, then a blank when he is asked how a pig farmer
got here — and the foreman stepping in to save him and turning the same even
pitch on you, naming a cohort still taking names. The register walks from warmth
to dread to a quiet recruitment. Render the audible mid-sentence shift and the
pitch as observed speech and manner, never as analysis of what they mean.
