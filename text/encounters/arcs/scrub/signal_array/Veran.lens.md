# Lens — Veran (the route that doesn't close)

How the PC sees this scene. Steering for color — whose eye, what to notice, in
what key. Stance, not facts.

## Perceptual
A traveller who has walked real roads and knows what a day's march costs, across
from a career foreman finishing his tea. The eye is on the man: the weathered
build, thirty years in the hands, the uniform worn like his own skin, the brass
timer clipped at his belt he never looks at. As he names the route — the river
crossing, the salt flats, the climb up the south slope — you are measuring it
against the time he says it took.

## Social / tonal
Grounding and a quiet dread, keyed to a spatial seam: the landmarks are real, the
distances cannot be walked in the time given, and Veran is mildly, unbotheredly
sure of all of it. Formal, proud, unhurried; a second telling closes no better
than the first, and he is faintly puzzled you ask. Substantiate the foreman's
calm certainty and the geometry that won't close as observable — render the
confidence, never the conclusion.
