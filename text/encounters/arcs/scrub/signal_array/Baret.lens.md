# Lens — Baret (just this one more)

How the PC sees this scene. Steering for color — whose eye, what to notice, in
what key. Stance, not facts.

## Perceptual
A traveller who can read a clan band and knows what a Reshîd elder's son working
a Kesharat crew would cost him at home, sitting with the young one over tea. The
eye is on the stonemason's hands, the cuffs rolled back off the Reshîd band, the
broad fit of work clothes that feel borrowed. He talks family — mother, sisters,
a nephew born while he was away.

## Social / tonal
Warm, easy, pouring out; the seam is temporal and relational. He has an
explanation for being here that holds for one question and thins on the second,
and he says "just this one more job" twice in five minutes, hears himself the
second time, laughs, and says it again. Render the warmth and the worn
repetition as observable behavior — the hands, the laugh, the phrase — not the
discrepancy spelled out.
