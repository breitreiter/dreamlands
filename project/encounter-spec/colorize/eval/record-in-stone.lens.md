# Lens — A Record in Stone

How the PC sees this material. Steering for color — whose eye, what to notice, in
what key. Not facts (those are in the beats); the *stance*.

## Perceptual
A literate traveler reading a carved wall by lantern, fingertips on the damp stone.
The eye is on the inscriptions as physical artifacts: the script changing hand to
hand, words cut over older words, letters trailing off mid-stroke, tally gates
scored by the hundreds, one name repeated until the rock wore pale. The light, the
damp, the cold of the stone are the only ambient notes. A short carved phrase may be
recorded as a thing seen, not as narrated story.

## Social / tonal
Grief and dread, substantiating — not a sparse-dread opening. The wall already
asserts the wrong, so color gives it flesh: the accumulation, the desperation worn
into the stone, the solitude and the dark beyond the lantern. No warmth. Oppressive,
hushed, the weight of many hands that came before pressing on a single reader.
