## Notes

**Register mix:** Almost entirely dread/uncanny, with one aliveness line (#11, the ventilation current). The beat asserts alien architecture throughout; color's job here is to *substantiate* that assertion with concrete atomic observables rather than establish a normal baseline — so the dread-heavy ratio is correct per the exemplars' teaching ("Register is keyed to the beat, not fixed").

**Color pulled from sub-reveals:** The hub tableau ("the plaza is quiet and empty, save for Torben's sketching") contributes the statue, Torben's knuckle-tap, and the sourceless dome-light. The bulk of the material comes from The Commons (tables, dust, basins, drainage, dark panels, dome-light thinning), The Watchtower (stair stride, air, dome proximity, city scale), and The Exchange (sealed loading door). The Archive contributes only one line (Torben's silence while walking). This distribution is flagged in the .enc's FIXME beats.

**Hardest show/argue boundary:** "Nothing is a size or shape you'd expect it to be" (source text) is explicit editorializing and was not lifted as-is. Instead it was broken into specific atomics: the drainage basins (shape), the table spacing (distance), the stair stride (body). The gold lines name the concrete observable and stop.

**Sensory range:** Predominantly visual. Sound: one (knuckle on plinth). Bodily/proprioceptive: two (stride, air change). No smell lines; the source text doesn't offer one cleanly. Acceptable gap given the material.

**PC frame:** The sourceless dome-light and sealed industrial door carry implicit PC-frame weight (renaissance-era person; cold even light is outside their experience) but neither line editorializes on that fact. Kept substance-only; a model could surface the PC-frame implication in generation.
