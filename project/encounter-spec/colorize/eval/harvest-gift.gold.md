## Notes

**Register mix:** Predominantly aliveness (the field, the light, the smell, the breeze) plus a cluster of human-strain lines keyed to the grandfather's failing body and the girl's cracking bravado. One warmth line (the grandmother's food). No supernatural register — this scene has none. The "dread" slot from the spec maps cleanly to `strain` here: social/emotional pressure, not wrongness. The ratio is roughly 5 aliveness : 5 strain : 1 warmth : 2 grounding, which tracks the beat's work: establish a warm pastoral baseline, then surface the desperation that's being held just under.

**Show/argue boundary:** Two lines needed care. "The hitch in his back between cuts, the pause, one hand pressed to his spine" is three observables in sequence — defensible as one held frame of the same action, but borderline; a curator could split it. "The small crack in her voice, swallowed almost before it lands" includes "almost before it lands," which is mild editorializing — could be cut to "a crack in her voice, quickly swallowed" or even just "her voice cracking once." Left in because the swallowing is itself a visible behavior (the action of suppression), not a reasoning clause. The "good stuff, her grandmother's" on the bread-and-cheese line carries a dossier risk (backstory via object) but is a direct fact from the source text, not an invention.

**Format notes:** All lines are noun-phrase-led per spec. Tags use `strain` where the spec's dread slot is filled by social pathos, per the task brief. The grandfather's fence line is tagged `environment` rather than `grandfather` because the fence is the observable anchor — the grandfather's act of bracing is implied by context and the prior line already has him as anchor.
