# Lens — A Gift for the Harvest

How the PC sees this material. Steering for color — whose eye, what to notice, in
what key. Not facts (those are in the beats); the *stance*.

## Perceptual
A traveler's eye on a working farm at the close of day: the ripe field, the light
going, and the labour of it — the old man's failing body at the far edge, the dull
sickle, the girl's stance in the road. Look at hands, posture, the work, the
weather. Plain rural world; nothing strange here to name.

## Social / tonal
Human strain, no supernatural — this is the off-key register here. A child's
bravado stretched over real desperation: planted feet, balled fists, a voice that
cracks and is swallowed. Warm pastoral baseline (grain, amber light, bread offered)
with the strain showing through it, and the grandfather's failing body carrying the
weight the girl won't say. The ring reads to her only as *guild folk have coin or
time*. Render the desperation as observable behavior, never as stated emotion.
