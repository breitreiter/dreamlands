## Notes

**Register mix:** Dread-heavy and substantiating, not sparse. This is correct for the beat. The scene's job is not to establish a baseline normal before introducing wrongness — it opens *in* wrongness (lost, mired, desolate) and the shack is the first relief. The false-warmth lines (stew smell, fire, her practical warning) are the lure, bracketed on both sides by dread observables. The ratio here runs roughly 7 dread : 5 aliveness : 3 warmth — that's appropriate because the beat already carries a declared dread charge (bones on eaves, something in the shack) and color's work is to substantiate, not seed.

**Hardest show/argue boundary call:** The source prose leans heavily on constructed contrast — "eyes that are, against all expectation, perfectly clear," the shrug carrying "the weight of absolute indifference to your survival," and "air so thick with rot it coats the back of your throat like oil." Each of those is a simile or an argued anomaly (the "against all expectation" framing is the tell). The hardest extraction was the eyes line: the original argues the wrongness ("against all expectation") rather than naming it. De-argued: drop the framing clause, keep the observable — pale, clear, amused against a wrecked face. The "not look back" beat was also a temptation: the original just states it flat and it lands fine, but adding any clause about *why* it's wrong (e.g. "she doesn't look back — as if she already knows") would tip it into argue territory. Left it flat.

**Format notes:** The bones-clicking line carries a small compound ("in a breeze the PC cannot feel") — this is borderline but retained because the breeze-absence is the observable, not an argument; it's what you'd note if you were standing there. The stew smell split across two lines (the cut-through and the stomach-clench) because those are two distinct sensory beats at different altitudes and collapsing them would lose the body-response, which is part of the warmth-as-lure texture.
