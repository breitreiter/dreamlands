## Notes

**Register mix:** strongly mundane/aliveness — arrival exhaustion, the trade-goods clutter, idle animals, the bureaucratic snag. These establish the baseline-normal this opening requires. Dread is a thin seam at the end: the ring's weight is the single off-note, and it lands because everything before it is ordinary. Ratio is approximately 8:2 innocent-to-dread, consistent with the exemplars' opening logic.

**Hardest judgment calls:**
- "the caravan idles in the road like a beast made to kneel" — source text is pure voice (simile). De-voiced to the observable: caravan halted, mules in the traces, neither moving nor freed. The simile is dropped entirely; the static fact remains.
- "the wind worrying the banners" — "worrying" is voice; de-voiced to banners moving in the wind. Kept because the motion is the observable fact the simile was pointing at.
- The ring observable is split into two lines (touch = the wrong weight; sight = the ring itself). Either could stand alone; two lines give the curator options and cover both senses the author used.

**Show/argue boundary:** no explanatory clauses in the source text to filter. The original narration is already fairly spare — the main voice-stripping work is the beast-simile and "worrying." No "despite," "not X but Y," or "as if" constructions to remove.

**Format:** the source `.enc` is short — four sentences of narration before the first choice. Ten lines reaches the lower end of the 10–18 target; this is appropriate given the actual density of the text.
