# Lens — Aldgate (Intro)

How the PC sees this material. Steering for color — whose eye, what to notice, in
what key. Not facts (those are in the beats); the *stance*.

## Perceptual
A road-worn traveler at the end of a long, hard caravan crossing, arriving footsore
at an Imperial city gate. The eye is tired and arrival-fixed: the trade goods piled
in the road, the idle mules, the bureaucratic snag at the gate, the banners above
it. Period frame — Imperial functionaries, frock coats and seals, crates and cloth;
no modern terms.

## Social / tonal
An opening: color's job is to establish baseline normal — the ordinary bustle and
grind of a gate, weeks of travel settling out of the body. The PC keeps to
themselves (they decline the caravan master's plea; their own troubles are enough).
The single off-note, a thin seam in an otherwise mundane scene, is the unfamiliar
weight on their hand — a signet ring they do not remember putting on. Dread stays a
minority; the ring is the one wrong thing.
