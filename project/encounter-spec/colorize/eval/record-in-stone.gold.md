**Show/argue boundary — the oath fragments:** the two quoted passages sit at the edge of the show/argue line. The rule applied: a short fragment is included only when it functions as a *physical artifact* (carved words observed on stone) rather than as narrated plot. **Show/argue boundary — "the stone wore pale around it":** "wore pale" is a light perception inference but reads as a visual fact (the stone's surface color where a chisel has worked it repeatedly).
